## Final Verdict

**Status**: APPROVED WITH RESERVATIONS

**Summary**: The feature does exactly what it set out to do and does it narrowly: the
repository-root escape is bounded by a guard this audit could not defeat, `assertContained`
is provably untouched, exactly one artifact in all of `src/` declares the non-default root,
and FC-13's dogfood byte-identity was independently re-proven with the only drift anywhere
being the sanctioned header comment. All 43 contract items and all 31 planned tests pass;
the suite is at 633 + 71 = 704 passing with the one pre-existing, deliberately-preserved
failure. The reservations are one traceability defect (`tasks.md` never updated) and five
small, concrete polish items — none of them a contract violation or a broken guarantee.
